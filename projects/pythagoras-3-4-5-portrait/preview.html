See that 3² + 4² = 5² by counting the unit squares built on each side of a right triangle.

• Here is a right triangle with legs 3 and 4 and hypotenuse 5.
• The square on the leg of length 3 holds 9 unit squares.
• The square on the leg of length 4 holds 16 unit squares.
• The square on the hypotenuse, of length 5, holds 25 unit squares.
• Add the two smaller areas: 9 plus 16 is 25.
• So 3 squared plus 4 squared equals 5 squared.

Result: 3² + 4² = 5² (9 + 16 = 25).
