How steep is x³ at x = 1? · 480×854 · 20.07 s

See why the slope of x³ at x = 1 is 3, by watching secant lines turn into the tangent line.

• Here is the curve f(x) = x³.
• We want the steepness of the curve exactly at the point P = (1, 1).
• Pick a second point Q, a distance h to the right, and draw the line through P and Q.
• The slope of this secant line works out to Slope = 3 + 3h + h².
• Now slide Q toward P by shrinking h.
• With h = 0.01 the secant slope is already 3.03.
• In the limit, the secant lines become the tangent line.
• So the derivative at x = 1 is 3: the slope of the tangent line.

Result: f′(1) = 3 for f(x) = x³.
